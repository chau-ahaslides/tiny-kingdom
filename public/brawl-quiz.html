❔ How to play

  • 👆 Tap anywhere to send your knight there.
  • ❓ When two fighters meet, both get the same quiz. Answer first and right to win a random prize: a heal or lasting strength — plus the first strike.
  • ❌ A wrong answer costs HP, but you may try another option.
  • ⚔ Then the swords decide — the fight is partly luck. The prize tilts the odds toward the quick thinker, it does not guarantee the win.
  • ⏳ No right answer in 10 s → straight to the sword fight. You are pulled to your rival and swing automatically; each strike opens a short escape window (blue bar) to run.
  • 🔒 Nobody can attack a duelling pair.
