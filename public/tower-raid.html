🏰 How to defend

  • 👹Gremlins march down the road to the Attention Tower. Every one that gets through bites it. When it's gone, the game is over.
  • ❓Each wave starts with a quiz at the bottom of your screen. Answer right and you get a gun to drag onto the board, with your name on it.
  • 🔥Right again next wave = a bigger gun: ballista → cannon → turret → catapult → 💎 crystal turret. Six in a row = ⚡ God Mode: you refill the emptiest guns on the board (2 at streak 6, 3 at 7, and so on). Wrong or too slow = no gun, streak lost.
  • 🪫Guns carry limited ammo and vanish when empty, so keep answering and keep placing.
  • 👆One finger turns the board, pinch to zoom, ⌖ resets the view.
