The gremlins march the moment a wave starts. Each wave: 15 s to answer the quiz, then 5 s to place guns, then 5 s to watch — and the next horde marches in on the clock, whatever is left of this one. Right = place a gun anywhere. Every right answer in a row makes the next gun bigger: ballista → cannon → turret → catapult → crystal turret. Six in a row is ⚡ God Mode: it refills the emptiest guns on the board, two at streak 6, three at 7, and so on. Wrong = no gun and the streak is gone.
Every gun carries a limited load of ammo and vanishes when it runs dry — keep placing new ones.
The tower has 10 attention points; each gremlin that gets through bites some off. Hold as many waves as you can.
