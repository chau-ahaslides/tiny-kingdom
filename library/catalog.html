guide (llms.txt) · manifest.json · packs.json 
Every file lives at /<path>. Paths and metadata come from manifest.json:
  sfx/<pack>/<category>/<name>-<variant>.m4a   sounds (AAC, 44.1 kHz; play with Web Audio or <audio>)
  sprites/<pack>/...                              PNG sheets and frames (width/height in the manifest)
  models/<pack>/...                               glTF / GLB with textures beside them
  music/…  fonts/…                                  the odd track and font

Starter maps

One ready-made level per tileset pack, drawn live by aha-assets.js from the same files a game would load. Drag to pan.
